## Not an Official Product

MABIMS API is an independent project and is **not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially authorized by** the [Ministry of Religious Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia](https://kemenag.go.id) or MABIMS.

## Not Official Data

Data presented is sourced from publicly available Kemenag publications. Computed results (`mabims-computed`) are **algorithmic estimates** using [Neo MABIMS criteria](https://mui.or.id/baca/berita/mengenal-kriteria-hilal-mabims-standard-penentuan-awal-bulan-hijriyah-pemerintah-indonesia) and are **not official observations or government announcements**.

## Not a Religious Authority

This API is intended for **technical and development use**. For religious decisions (start of fasting, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha), always refer to official announcements from the Ministry of Religious Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia, determined through the [isbat session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon_sighting).

## Accuracy

Data is provided in good faith but **accuracy is not guaranteed**. It may contain errors or update delays. Always verify with official sources for important decisions.
