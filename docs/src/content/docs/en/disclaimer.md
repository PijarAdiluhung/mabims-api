## Not an Official Product

MABIMS API is an independent project and is **not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially authorized by** the Ministry of Religious Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia or MABIMS.

## Not Official Data

Data presented is sourced from publicly available Kemenag publications. Computed results (`mabims-computed`) are **algorithmic estimates** using Neo MABIMS criteria and are **not official observations or government announcements**.

## Not a Religious Authority

This API is intended for **technical and development use**. For religious decisions (start of fasting, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha), always refer to official announcements from the Ministry of Religious Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia, determined through the isbat session.

## Accuracy

Data is provided in good faith but **accuracy is not guaranteed**. It may contain errors or update delays. Always verify with official sources for important decisions.
